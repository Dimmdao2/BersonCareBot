The owner's newer decisions from 2026-09-05 supersede incompatible older prose:

1. There is ONE globally configured billing-period grid for every tariff. A period is data
   (`label`, positive month count, ordering, selectable/retired state), never a hardcoded code or
   a per-tariff enable flag. The platform admin may add arbitrary periods such as 3 or 9 months.
2. Every active tariff has its own monetary amount for EVERY globally selectable period. Different
   tariffs cannot expose different period sets. A period is first created non-selectable, prices
   are filled for all active tariffs, and activation is refused until that matrix is complete.
   Retirement is global and non-destructive; historical invoices/prices remain readable.
3. A tariff change, billing-period change, or subscription cancellation never rewrites or shortens
   the already-paid interval/snapshot. It takes effect only at `currentPeriodEndsAt`. Remove the
   older immediate/prorated tariff-upgrade path. Rank/"more expensive" metadata is explicitly out
   of scope.
4. SaaS payments from clinics to the platform are non-refundable. Remove/disable the actionable
   SaaS-refund API/UI/service path, while preserving historical ledger rows/read reporting. This
   does NOT affect patient-payment refunds performed by a clinic.
5. No wallet, account balance, store credit, mass access extension, or final clinic tariff-page
   redesign in this stage.
